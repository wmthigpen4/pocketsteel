Why your review matters

Every correction helps the engine get closer the first time.

Your edits create expert-labeled examples of the mistakes that matter: wrong chords, late changes, bad count-ins, misplaced downbeats, and incorrect split bars. I can synthesize those patterns to tune the engine and measure whether each new version needs less human correction.

Why I believe this is worth building

The starting point is already unusually strong.

In the comparisons I have run, this is already producing more useful, time-aligned country chord maps than the open-source tools, online services, and paid apps I could find. That is not a claim that it is perfect—your review is how I find and close the remaining gap.

The long-term goal is simple: every new lesson starts with a better draft, takes less of your time to approve, and reaches the Companion faster. You are reviewing a draft—not building the chart from scratch.
