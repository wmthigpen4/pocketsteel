# Amazing Grace guided lesson recording

- Composition: “Amazing Grace” (traditional hymn; public-domain composition).
- Source recording: “Amazing Grace 2011” by Kevin MacLeod (ISRC USUAN1100820).
- Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazing_Grace_2011_(ISRC_USUAN1100820).mp3>
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported, <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>.
- Required attribution: “Amazing Grace 2011” Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under CC BY 3.0.
- Steel Guitar Rag lesson changes: the first 16-bar verse was extracted, transposed from C to G, and slowed from 79 to 76 BPM; a two-bar count-in was added; the lesson timeline and synchronized lyrics were hand-authored. No endorsement by Kevin MacLeod is implied.
- Instrumentation in the licensed recording: clarinet, pipe organ, and piano. There is no steel-guitar part.
