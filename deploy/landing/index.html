Why it works

Instrument logic first, sources when they matter.

Steel Guitar RAG is being built from real steel-guitar material: forum discussions, lesson transcripts, gear conversations, copedent notes, manuals, tab references, and player experience gathered over decades.

  • Validated Explorer data handles static grips, chord locations, scale paths, notes, and voicing checks as fretboard-first teaching surfaces.
  • Source-aware SGF and curated guidance can support answers when relevant.
  • Long discussions are broken into smaller musical ideas and searched by meaning, not just exact wording.
  • The goal is a calm answer that knows where it came from and how it lays out on the guitar.
