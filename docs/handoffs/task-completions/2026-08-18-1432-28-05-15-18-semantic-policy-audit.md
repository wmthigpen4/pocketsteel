## Policy architecture

- `local_answer_policy_guardrail()` is isolated from ordinary answer routing and returns only non-negotiable policy reasons:
  - unsafe or unbounded output;
  - sensitive personal attributes;
  - specific private biography.
- Local policy evaluates the current question only, so a stale prior turn does not permanently taint a later ordinary request.
- When local policy applies:
  - semantic planner calls are forbidden;
  - corpus entity/context probes are forbidden;
  - retrieval is forbidden even when conversation context is present;
  - specific existing policy copy is preserved for personal/biographical requests;
  - unsafe/unbounded requests preserve the existing unsafe/large-output explanation.
- Ordinary conceptual and public-biography questions return no local policy decision and continue to semantic authority.
