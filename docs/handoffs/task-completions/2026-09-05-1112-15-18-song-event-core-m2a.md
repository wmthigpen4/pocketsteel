## Contract Decisions Now Executable

- Milliseconds are authoritative for v1 selection.
- Root `media` clocks and mapped `window` clocks are distinct.
- Sections, measures, and phrases keep their own identities.
- Chords and steel performances use one typed envelope but separate bodies.
- Steel events may reference a containing chord and a reusable position.
- Held controls, articulations, and transitions remain separate concepts.
- Event ordering is `(clockId, startMs, endMs, trackId, id)`.
- One logical track cannot overlap; different tracks can.
- Current and next event IDs are nullable at all required boundaries.
- Product approval, provenance, release, and teaching presentation remain
  outside the shared document.
